Recipe 1: Mexican Black Bean & Pepper Wraps with Side Salad

Serves: 2

Preparation: 15 minutes

Cooking: 15–20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 x 400g tin black beans
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1/2 yellow pepper
  • 1 medium red onion
  • 2 medium tortilla wraps
  • 60g mature cheddar
  • 4 tbsp salsa
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garlic granules
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional chilli for Dave
  • Mixed lettuce, cucumber and cherry tomatoes
  • Olive oil or salad dressing

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large frying pan.
  2. Add onion and peppers and cook for 6–8 minutes.
  3. Stir in paprika, cumin and garlic granules.
  4. Add black beans and water.
  5. Mash a few beans to bind the filling.
  6. Cook for 4–5 minutes until piping hot.
  7. Divide filling between the wraps.
  8. Add 30g cheddar and 2 tbsp salsa to each.
  9. Fold and toast in a dry frying pan for 1–2 minutes each side.
  10. Serve with fresh side salad.

Our verdict ❤️

Enjoyed by both Dave and Lisa. One generously filled wrap each is sufficient. Side salad is preferred; sweet potato wedges aren't needed. Dave can add extra chilli separately.

Favourite – make again!
